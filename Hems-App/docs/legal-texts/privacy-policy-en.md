This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our Netzr mobile application.

## 1. Data We Collect

We collect the following types of personal data:

- **Personal Information:** Name, surname, email address, phone number
- **Account Data:** Login credentials, user preferences
- **Energy Data:** Solar production, battery status, grid import/export data, energy consumption patterns
- **Device Telemetry:** Inverter status, device serial numbers, firmware versions, real-time power metrics
- **Location Data:** Home address, device installation location (for accurate energy monitoring)
- **Camera Data:** We access your device camera solely to scan QR codes for device registration. No images or video are captured, stored, or transmitted.
- **Usage Data:** App usage patterns, feature interactions, error logs

## 2. Purpose of Data Processing

We process your data for the following purposes:

- To provide and maintain the Netzr energy management service
- To monitor and optimize your energy production and consumption
- To send notifications about system status, alerts, and updates
- To improve our services and develop new features
- To comply with legal obligations
- To communicate with you regarding your account and support requests

## 3. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal data based on:

- Your consent when you create an account and accept these terms
- Performance of a contract (providing our services to you)
- Legal obligations (GDPR - General Data Protection Regulation)
- Legitimate interests (improving our services, preventing fraud)

## 4. Third-Party Sharing

We may share your data with:

- **Cloud Service Providers:** For secure data storage and processing
- **Analytics Providers:** To improve app performance (anonymized data)
- **Energy Grid Operators:** When required for grid integration
- **Government Authorities:** When required by law

We never sell your personal data to third parties.

## 5. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for:

- **Account data:** As long as your account is active, plus 2 years after deletion
- **Energy data:** 5 years for historical analysis and reporting
- **Device telemetry:** 2 years for service improvement
- **Usage logs:** 1 year for troubleshooting

You may request earlier deletion by contacting us.

## 6. Your Rights

Under GDPR, you have the right to:

- **Access:** Request a copy of your personal data
- **Rectification:** Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
- **Erasure:** Request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten")
- **Portability:** Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
- **Objection:** Object to processing based on legitimate interests
- **Restriction:** Request limitation of processing
- **Withdraw Consent:** Withdraw your consent at any time

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@grpeurope.com

## 7. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including:

- Encryption in transit and at rest
- Secure authentication mechanisms
- Regular security audits
- Access controls and monitoring

## 10. Device Permissions

Our app requires certain device permissions to function properly:

- **Camera:** Used exclusively to scan QR codes for registering energy devices (inverters, batteries). We do not capture, store, or transmit any photos or videos. Camera access is only active during the QR scanning process.

- **Location:** Used to determine your home location for accurate energy monitoring, time zone settings, and regional energy data.

- **Notifications:** Used to send alerts about energy production, battery status, system warnings, and important updates.

You can manage these permissions at any time through your device settings.
